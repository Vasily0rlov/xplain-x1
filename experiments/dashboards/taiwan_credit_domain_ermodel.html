How this was derived

The flat ML table has one row per observation. The load-bearing structure was derived from the column semantics: repeating indexed column groups (PAY_0..6, BILL_AMT1..6, PAY_AMT1..6 for Taiwan) are a repeating group ⇒ a child table (MONTHLY_STATEMENT). Bike's columns split cleanly into a calendar dimension, a weather lookup, and per-hour measures ⇒ a star schema. Domain knowledge supplies the entity names; the normalisation is what makes the certified collinearity findings legible — they fall out of the domain structure.
